Project Skyscraper - Neighbouring Windows

Overview

This page plots the voxel coordinates of only neighbouring systems reported by the RecallDreams.dev "Echoes of the Threshold" demo terminal, as they are in the Euclid galactic map.

Systems are associated with operators as follows:

  • If the operator's prime has one or more neighbours (anagram primes), then the operator's portal coordinates are taken from the neighbour's terminal response (i.e. the system visible from their neighbour's window, with the operator's name attributed).
  • If the operator's prime has no neighbours, then the coordinates are taken from their terminal response (i.e. the system visible from their own window).

It is uncertain at this time whether operators with no neighbours are located at the system in their own terminal response, or at another system which is not reported by the terminal.



Example - One Neighbour

> 1009

NeighbourOne, right? Nice to meet you! If you look out your window, you will see NeighbourTwo there: Goggrin-Derpa [13254C2D3D9F]

> 9001

NeighbourTwo, right? Nice to meet you! If you look out your window, you will see NeighbourOne there: Maclabet-Xev [2045CF423D51]

With this terminal output:

  • Operator NeighbourOne (prime 1009) would be associated with system Maclabet-Xev
  • Operator NeighbourTwo (prime 9001) would be associated with system Goggrin-Derpa.


Example - No Neighbour

> 5569

Neighbourless, right? Nice to meet you! If you look out your window, you will see Aardabarg XII [314D695C1001]

With this terminal output, operator Neighbourless (prime 5569) would be associated with system Aardabarg XII. For this page, neighbourless operators are not displayed.
